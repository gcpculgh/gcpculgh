Who can join

Three months to your first loan.

Open to every registered pharmacist in Ghana, plus staff at pharmacy-related institutions. Save consistently for 3 months and you're eligible to borrow - reviewed by an elected Loans Committee, not a call centre.

  • 3-month rule. Save consistently, then apply - no waiting years to build trust.
  • You elect the board. Directors, Loans Committee, and Supervisory Committee are all voted in by members.
  • Transparent appraisal. Every application is reviewed against CUA's published risk guidelines - no black box.
