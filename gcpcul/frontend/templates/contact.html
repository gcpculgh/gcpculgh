Member Support & FAQs

Quick answers

How do I check my savings or dividend balance? expand_more
Dial *899*334# on any network to check balances instantly, no data required. You'll need your 4-digit USSD PIN; request one from the office if you haven't set it up yet.
How long does a loan application take? expand_more
Fast-Track loans (within 3x your savings) are typically approved within 48 hours. Applications above that limit go to the Loans Committee and depend on their meeting schedule. Contact the office for the next sitting date.
Can I submit forms without visiting the office? expand_more
Yes. Download the form you need from the Document Vault, complete it, and send it via WhatsApp or email. An officer will confirm receipt and tell you if anything else is needed.
Who qualifies for membership? expand_more
All registered pharmacists in Ghana, staff at pharmacy-related institutions, and others who meet our pre-qualification criteria. See Who We Are for the full common bond, or call the office to confirm your eligibility.
