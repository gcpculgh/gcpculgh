Common Questions

Loans & Repayment FAQ

How is flat-rate interest calculated on my loan? expand_more
Flat annual interest is calculated on the total original principal borrowed for the entire duration of the loan. For example, a 15% flat rate on GH¢10,000.00 over 12 months equals GH¢1,500.00 in total interest, divided equally across 12 monthly installments.
What happens if my requested loan exceeds 3x my savings? expand_more
Loans up to 3x your regular savings balance are processed as Fast-Track facilities without external guarantors. If you require an amount exceeding 3x your savings, your application is routed to the Loans Committee and requires either a qualified member guarantor or acceptable collateral.
Are there any hidden processing fees or insurance charges? expand_more
No. All rates published by GCPCUL are transparent. At the time of loan disbursement, a nominal one-time administrative processing fee and CUA Loan Protection Plan (LPP) insurance premium are deducted upfront, guaranteeing your family never inherits debt in the event of death or permanent disability.
