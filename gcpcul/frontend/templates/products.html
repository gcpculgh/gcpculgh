Common Questions

Loans & Shares FAQ

What do I need to qualify for a loan?
Active GCPCUL membership with the minimum required share capital (GH¢500.00), at least 3 months of savings history, and a guarantor or collateral appropriate to the loan tier — savings-backed Fast-Track loans typically need no external guarantor.
How long does loan approval take?
Fast-Track loans guaranteed by your own savings are typically approved within 48 hours. Committee-reviewed loans (business, housing, vehicle) usually take 5–10 working days depending on documentation and guarantor verification.
Is my loan insured if something happens to me?
Yes. All approved loans are covered under the CUA Loan Protection Plan (LPP), which settles the outstanding balance in the event of a member's death or permanent disability, protecting your family from inherited debt.
Can I repay my loan early without a penalty?
Yes. Members may settle a loan ahead of schedule at any time. Because interest is calculated on a flat basis, early repayment reduces your future obligation but does not refund interest already accrued — ask a loan officer for an exact settlement figure.
How is my dividend on share capital calculated?
The dividend rate is proposed by the Board and approved by members at the Annual General Meeting, based on the Union's audited annual surplus. Dividends are paid proportionally to each member's average share balance for the year.
