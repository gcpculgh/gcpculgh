Featured Announcement

2026 Dividend Payouts Declared: A Record 18% Yield for Active Members

GCPCUL Finance Committee Official Board Communication
schedule
August 2, 2026 • 8 min read

The Board of Directors of the Ghana Co-operative Pharmacists' Credit Union Limited (GCPCUL) is proud to announce a historic milestone for our cooperative. Following a highly successful 2025 fiscal year, marked by disciplined risk management and robust loan portfolio growth, the Annual General Meeting has officially approved an 18% dividend yield on all active share capital.

How Your Dividend is Calculated

Dividends are calculated based on your average share balance throughout the fiscal year. To reward consistency, members who maintained their share capital without deductions received the highest proportional returns. This 18% yield significantly outperforms current commercial fixed-deposit rates, validating our cooperative model.

"Our strength lies in our collective equity. This record payout is a direct result of our members' commitment to steady saving and responsible borrowing."
— GCPCUL Board Chairman

When Will Payouts Hit Your Account?

Distributions will be automatically credited to members' regular savings accounts starting the second week of August. Members can track their updated balances seamlessly via the official GCPCUL Mobile App or by using our USSD shortcode *899*334#.

We encourage all members to consider reinvesting their dividends into their share capital to compound their future returns and unlock higher Fast-Track borrowing limits for the upcoming year.
