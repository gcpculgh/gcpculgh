Inception & Evolution

Our Cooperative Journey

1985
The journey of GCPCUL commenced in 1983 when the idea of embracing the credit union concept was first conceived. By May 29, 1985, this vision became a reality as we were officially registered as the Ghana Co-operative Pharmaceuticals Credit Union Ltd. At that time, our association was limited exclusively to Pharmacists who were members of the Ghana Co-operative Pharmaceuticals Ltd.
1998
In 1997, we achieved autonomy from our parent organization, GCPL. In 1998, during our Annual General Meeting, we officially adopted the name Ghana Co-operative Pharmacists' Credit Union Limited. This milestone marked the expansion of our common bond to encompass all registered Pharmacists in the country.
2009
We further broadened our common bond at our 23rd Annual General Meeting on Thursday, March 12, 2009, held at the GNAT Hall in Accra. It now includes non-Pharmacists working with pharmacy-related institutions and others who meet our pre-qualification criteria.

Legal & Compliance

Institutional Policies

Privacy & Data Protection Policy expand_more

Data Collection & Usage

GCPCUL operates under a strict zero-trust data mandate. We collect necessary KYC (Know Your Customer) information solely to verify identity and process financial disbursements as mandated by the Bank of Ghana and CUA.

Data Security

We never store payment PINs or sensitive authentication details on our web servers. All document uploads via our Digital Vault are end-to-end encrypted. Access is strictly limited to authorized Management and the Loans Committee via secure Maker-Checker workflows.

Terms of Service & Cooperative Rules expand_more

Membership Obligations

By registering, members agree to maintain a minimum Share Capital of GH¢500.00 and a minimum regular savings account balance of GH¢100.00. Members must contribute consistently for three (3) months before becoming eligible for any loan facility.

Dividend Distribution

Dividends are declared at the Annual General Meeting based on the audited surplus. Payouts are directly proportional to a member's average share capital maintained throughout the fiscal year without deductions.

CUA Affiliation & LPP Insurance expand_more

The Ghana Co-operative Pharmacists' Credit Union Limited (GCPCUL) is a fully registered and compliant affiliate of the Ghana Co-operative Credit Unions Association (CUA). Reg No: CO-OP/847.

Every loan disbursed is automatically covered by the CUA Loan Protection Plan (LPP). This premium is deducted upfront, ensuring that in the tragic event of death or total permanent disability, the outstanding loan balance is fully written off by CUA, completely protecting the member's family and next-of-kin from inherited debt.
