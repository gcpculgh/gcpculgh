Who Qualifies to be a Member?

Open to Ghana's Pharmaceutical & Healthcare Community

All registered Pharmacists in Ghana, non-Pharmacists working with pharmacy-related institutions, and others who have been pre-qualified can join GCPCUL under our democratic cooperative structure.

  • Three (3) Months Rule: After joining the Credit Union, a member qualifies for their first loan after 3 months of consistent savings.
  • Elected Member Governance: Guided by an elected Board of Directors, Supervisory Committee, Loans Committee, and Education & Editorial Committee.
  • Transparent Appraisal: Applications are appraised by the elected Loans Committee under strict CUA risk management guidelines.
